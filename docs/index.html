<eat-sleep-code /> Device Screenshot Tool

Overview

A desktop app that captures screenshots of any website — including internal, VPN-only, and localhost apps — across a full set of device presets, including the current iPhone and Pixel models, and a handful of desktop/tablet sizes.

  • Runs on your machine, so intranet, staging, and localhost URLs are reachable
  • Portrait and landscape, viewport-only or full-page capture
  • Sign in once per internal app; the session is reused for future captures
  • Custom device presets, alongside the built-in list
